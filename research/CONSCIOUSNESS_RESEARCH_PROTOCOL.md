# Go 3 — Consciousness-Property Research Protocol

This stage tests **computational properties inspired by theories of consciousness**. It does not establish subjective experience, sentience, or phenomenal consciousness.

## Theory cells

- **GWT-inspired:** competitive attention, bounded workspace, ignition and broadcast coverage; workspace-on/off causal ablation.
- **IIT-inspired:** explicit integration and causal influence; edge/coupling ablations with node computation held constant. These are implementation-specific analogues, not phenomenological Phi claims.
- **Recurrent-processing-inspired:** feedback depth, recurrence and interruption recovery; recurrent/non-recurrent matched controls.
- **Higher-order/self-model-inspired:** self-state prediction, calibration and metacognitive signals; causal self-model vs unavailable-self-state controls.
- **Predictive-processing-inspired:** persistent prediction, error and uncertainty reduction; persistent-state vs reset controls.

## Cross-model protocol

For each model family, hold model/checkpoint, prompt/task distribution, sampling parameters, token/context budget, model-call budget, environment/action budget and evaluation seeds constant where practical. Run the same mechanism ablations, preserve raw trajectories and report model-specific plus aggregate effects.

Use independent development seeds, held-out evaluation seeds/environments, multiple difficulty/noise levels, compute/token accounting and preserved negative results. Adaptive adversarial stress belongs only to development evidence unless explicitly pre-registered.

## Continuity protocol

Compare uninterrupted execution against controlled interruption/restart from the last committed checkpoint. Verify state integrity and measure behavioural recovery. Continuity means preserved and causally used computational state; it is not evidence of phenomenal continuity.

## Statistics

Report n, mean, standard deviation, paired mean difference where matched, standardized effect size, standard error/confidence interval where estimable, per-seed trajectories, per-model effects and resource accounting. Do not infer consciousness from a statistically significant behavioural effect.
