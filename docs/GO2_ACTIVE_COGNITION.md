# Go 2 — Embodied / Active Cognition

Go 2 extends Go 1 with active-inference-inspired action and observation selection, valuation, homeostasis and persistent identity/autobiographical state.

Candidate actions are scored using expected utility, information gain, uncertainty reduction and risk. The selected action is only a **proposal**: executable authority remains with NSA policy and capability enforcement.

Homeostatic state tracks energy, uncertainty, arousal and stability. Valuation updates action preferences from observed expected value. Identity carries a stable identifier, commitments, bounded autobiographical history and a continuity metric.

## Falsifiable hypotheses

- H7: epistemically valuable actions reduce uncertainty more efficiently than random selection at equal action budget.
- H8: homeostatic state improves resource allocation and recovery under sustained pressure.
- H9: persistent autobiographical state improves long-horizon continuity against an equal-context non-persistent control.
- H10: Go 1 recurrence plus active information acquisition produces larger gains than either mechanism alone.

These are behavioural hypotheses, not evidence of subjective experience.
